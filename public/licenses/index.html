Factory ammo library

The factory ammo figures (velocity, test barrel, BC, bullet weight and length) are the manufacturers' own published values, entered by hand from each maker's product page; each entry records its source and the date it was checked. Brand and product names are trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to identify products. No endorsement by any manufacturer is implied. Makers can change their specifications at any time.

Velocities adjusted for your barrel length are estimates (a per-inch rule of thumb based on published barrel cut-down tests). Real rifles commonly differ by 50 fps or more. Confirm on target and true your data.
